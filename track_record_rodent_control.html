Continuity of client relationships

Relationship duration is the most reliable quality indicator in maintenance services. Of the 728 rodent control clients:

  • 143 clients were served in five or more separate years
  • 32 clients in ten or more years
  • the longest-standing relationship spans 18 consecutive years (2009–2026)
  • 84 active maintenance programmes are currently in execution

Methodology

Work is carried out using approved products and tamper-resistant stations, under a written programme for each site. A numbered station network is applied, with positions mapped on floor plans, so that every control point is unambiguously identifiable at each visit. Indoor and outdoor bait stations, glue monitoring stations and mechanical traps are used, with the method selected case by case — in food handling areas, non-toxic monitoring methods are preferred.

Documentation & traceability

Every visit is documented with a per-station rodent control record (bait condition, findings, actions) and is accompanied by a treatment certificate listing the products used, their active substances and authorisation numbers. All documentation is delivered digitally and remains available to the client in a personal digital folder, holding the full inspection history per station — material directly usable in hygiene audits and in HACCP/ISO systems.
